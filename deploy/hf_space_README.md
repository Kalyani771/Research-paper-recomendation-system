# ScholarMatch: Research Paper Recommendation System

Semantic research paper recommendations: papers are ranked by meaning with
Sentence-Transformers (all-MiniLM-L6-v2) and cosine similarity, across a
41,105-paper Kaggle arXiv dataset plus live IEEE Xplore, OpenAlex, arXiv and
Semantic Scholar results.

Major project, Department of Computer Science and Engineering, RGUKT Basar.
