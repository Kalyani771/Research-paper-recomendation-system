How it works

Ranked by meaning, from start to finish

Keyword search misses papers that describe the same idea in other words. ScholarMatch compares meanings instead.

  1. 1

    Understand the query

    A MiniLM sentence-transformer turns your text into a 384-dimension vector that captures what it means. Technical terms like NLP, IoT or LLM are kept intact.

  2. 2

    Gather candidates

    IEEE Xplore, OpenAlex, arXiv and Semantic Scholar are searched in parallel, alongside {{ "{:,}".format(paper_count) }} pre-encoded dataset papers.

  3. 3

    Rank by similarity

    Every paper's title and abstract is encoded with the same model and scored by cosine similarity, giving one ranking across all sources.

  4. 4

    Verify the metadata

    Authors, venue, DOI and links come straight from the publishing platform or Crossref. Nothing is guessed.
